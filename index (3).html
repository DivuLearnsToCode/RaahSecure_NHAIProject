How is the Score calculated?

The Corruption Probability Score (CPS) is a weighted algorithmic score derived entirely from publicly available government data. It flags systemic anomalies — not individuals.

Data Sources

  • NHAI/PWD Financial Records (RTI disclosures)
  • CAG Audit Reports — Ministry of Road Transport
  • ISRO Bhuvan — GIS Satellite Road Imagery
  • GeM Portal Tender Data (Open Government Data)
  • PFMS — Public Financial Management System
  • State PWD Contractor Registry and Blacklist

Scoring Thresholds

  • Budget Overrun (25%): Above 15% overrun → 50+; above 30% → 75+
  • Repair Frequency (20%): 3+ repairs on same stretch in 2 years → 80+
  • Tender Transparency (20%): Open bids → 0; direct awards → 100
  • Quality Compliance (15%): IRC material test failure rate
  • Audit Compliance (10%): Unresolved CAG objections ratio
  • Contractor Status (10%): Pending cases, debarment history

For civic transparency only. All data is publicly accessible.
